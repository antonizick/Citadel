# 2026-07-03 — Trusted Resources Pipeline Rework (Complete)

## Problem statement
Trusted resource reports were low quality (1 story + apologies) because the pipeline **searched for** the source URL instead of **fetching** it. `run_resource()` passed the feed/homepage URL into DuckDuckGo as a query, and the LLM received ~10 search snippets while the prompt instructed it to "parse the RSS feed" and "follow links to full articles" — impossible task. Contributing: coding model (`qwen3.5-coder`) doing news synthesis; no real date filtering; dead `search_trusted_resource()` code.

## Design decision
Split pipelines by use case: **interests** stay search-based (topic monitoring); **resources** become fetch-based (pull specific sources). Both feed full text to a general instruct model (`qwen3.6:27b`) with proper context/output budgets.

## Build outcome

### Phases 0–6 (all complete)

**Phase 0** — Dependencies: `feedparser`, `trafilatura`, `lxml_html_clean` installed.

**Phase 1** — Fetch service (`app/services/fetch_service.py`):
- `fetch_source()` parses feeds with `feedparser`, auto-discovers feeds from homepages via `<link rel=alternate>`, fetches article pages concurrently with a browser UA (bypass 403s on obvious bots).
- Feed cap: 20 articles/run, 5 parallel fetches, fallback to feed-provided HTML on page-fetch failure.
- Result: 3 of 4 resource sources (THN, BleepingComputer, Krebs) yield **full article bodies** (60–175 KB HTML). Dark Reading hard-blocked by JS bot-management → teaser-only (120–144 chars, accepted degradation).

**Phase 2** — Extraction:
- `extract_stories()` → `trafilatura.extract()` per story, strips nav/ads/boilerplate. Fallback to `lxml.html.text_content()` for feed-only teasers (no new packages). Normalizes to `{title, url, published, text}`.
- Result: 2.5–18 KB clean article text per story.

**Phase 3** — Date filter:
- `filter_recent()` parses real publish dates via `dateutil`, drops verified-stale items only. **Keeps undated stories** (flagged "date unknown" by the model) rather than silently discarding them.
- Result: Live-proven on Krebs feed — kept 1 story from Jul 2, dropped 2 from Jun 23 & 18.

**Phase 4** — Rewire + model:
- `run_resource()` → `collect_stories(source, hours=_window_hours(schedule))`; removes `search_multi()`.
- `ai_service.summarize_resource()` refactored: new `_render_article_blocks()` feeds full article text (not snippets) to the LLM.
- **Critical fix:** `_ollama_chat()` explicitly sets `num_ctx` (Ollama silently drops input past ~4k default). Summaries use `SUMMARY_NUM_CTX=32768` + `SUMMARY_MAX_TOKENS=8192`.
- Model: `qwen3.6:27b` (confirmed in settings). Removed dead `search_trusted_resource()`.

**Phase 5** — E2E verification:
- Manual run of The Hacker News resource: 20 fetched → 19 filtered → **10 complete stories** with real IOCs (CVE-2025-9491, `maccyapp[.]com`, domain indicators). Report 20.2 KB, ends cleanly, no truncation. Runtime 196s at 32K context.
- Caught & fixed truncation bug (old 2048-token output ceiling → now 8192).

**Phase 6** — Interest enrichment (optional, built):
- `fetch_urls_text()` → fetches + extracts full article text for individual URLs (interest search results). Best-effort, non-fatal.
- `run_interest()` enriches top 5 search results with full article bodies (try/except wrapper).
- **Verified live** on real "CyberSecurity Daily Update" interest: report cited specific CVEs (CVE-2026-35616, CVE-2026-20230, CVE-2026-8451) and exact figures (430K firewalls, 110M credentials) — impossible from snippets alone.
- **Note:** that live test sent a real email to nick@antonizick.com and a Discord message (side effect of hitting a real, active interest).

## Model switching notes

- `num_ctx=32768` is a request; models with smaller max-context silently clamp.
- Current models: `qwen3.6:27b` & `qwen3.5` = 256K context ✅; `mistral-small` & `mistral` = 32K ✅.
- Rule: pick general instruct model with ≥32K context; watch VRAM on 27B+.
